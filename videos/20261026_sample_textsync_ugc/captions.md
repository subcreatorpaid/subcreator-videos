# Sample: text-sync loop (UGC with zero followers)

Reference sample for the "text-sync loop" format. Not scheduled. Facts: site/data/streams/ugc.json (checked 25 Oct 2026).

## Instagram
The list from the video. Save it before you film anything.

1. Film 3 samples on your phone this week
- Unboxing: open something you already own. First reaction, close-ups, 20-30 seconds.
- Review: 3 honest pros and 1 con.
- How-to: one problem the product solves, step by step.

2. Put them in one place
A simple link-in-bio or free portfolio page. Brands judge this, not your follower count.

3. Find paid briefs
- Apply to brand briefs on a UGC marketplace like Insense (insense.pro).
- List a UGC video service on Fiverr.
- Or pitch brands you already use, directly.

4. Send your first pitch (copy it)
"Hi [brand], I make short UGC videos for brands like yours. Here are 3 samples: [link]. I'd love to make a [unboxing / review / how-to] for [product]. My rate is $[your rate] per video, including [usage rights]. Can I send you a quick idea?"

Pay: beginners usually charge $50-$400 a video (Insense, Sep 2026). Rates vary by niche and usage rights, so agree price, usage and deadline in writing.
The catch: no brand says yes until it sees samples. Start there.
