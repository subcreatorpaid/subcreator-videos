## Instagram
4 places to find paid UGC work: 1) Billo: no follower minimum, creators in the US, UK and Canada. 2) JoinBrands: brands post briefs, you apply with your samples. 3) Fiverr: list UGC as a gig so brands can find you. 4) Pitch brands directly: email a sample made with a product you already use. The catch: Insense needs 1,000+ Instagram followers, and every brand wants samples first.
