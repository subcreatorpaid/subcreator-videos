## Instagram
Going viral won't pay you yet. YouTube Shorts ad money needs 1,000 subscribers and 10M Shorts views in 90 days (YouTube Help, checked 25 Oct 2026).

Fan money starts earlier:
- YouTube fan funding: from 500 subscribers, plus 3 public uploads in 90 days and 3,000 watch hours (12 months) or 3M Shorts views (90 days)
- Spotify Memberships: 100 listeners and 100 hours listened in 30 days, in 34 markets (Spotify, 20 Oct 2026)

The catch: fans need a reason to pay (bonus episodes, live chats), and each program has country and review rules.
