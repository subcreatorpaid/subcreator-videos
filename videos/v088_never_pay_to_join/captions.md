Never pay to join a brand program. Real deals pay you; if you pay first, it's a sales pitch. FTC red flags for fake ambassador managers: flattery, big name-drops, lookalike web addresses, early requests for bank or personal details. Find the brand's real site, contact them there, report fakes at reportfraud.ftc.gov. Free product instead of cash can be fine; paying never is.
