# 5 signs a brand deal is fake

## Instagram
The FTC's warning signs of a fake brand deal: 1) a "brand ambassador manager" messages you out of nowhere, 2) heavy flattery about your page or style, 3) they name-drop real people at the company, 4) they send links that only look official, 5) the big one: they ask for bank or personal details so payments can start. To check, contact the brand on a number you already know is real, never one they sent. Never share bank details in a DM. Report scams at reportfraud.ftc.gov.
