## Instagram
The FTC says a disclosure in your bio isn't enough. Put it in the post itself, where it's hard to miss: not only at the end, not behind "more", not lost in hashtags. In a video, say it and show it on screen, because some people watch without sound. Use clear words like "ad" or "sponsored"; skip "sp", "spon" or "collab". The catch: a platform's disclosure tool alone may not be enough, and US rules can apply even if you post from abroad.
