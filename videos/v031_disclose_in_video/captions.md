## Instagram
FTC Disclosures 101: in a video, disclose in the video itself, both out loud and on screen; in a live stream, repeat it. Don't hide it only in your profile, at the end, behind MORE, or mixed into hashtags. Use clear words (ad, sponsored), not sp/spon/collab. A platform's paid-partnership label may not be enough alone.
