## Instagram
Shutterstock pays contributors 15%-40% of what it earns per download, across 6 levels for images and video. On 1 January everyone returns to level 1 (Shutterstock help centre). Film what buyers search: hands typing, streets, food, everyday moments without faces or logos. The catch: small cut per download, so it adds up only with hundreds of clips.
