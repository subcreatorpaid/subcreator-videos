## Instagram
Your UGC Video Is An Ad Now. Did You Charge?

Making a UGC video and letting a brand run it as a paid ad are two different things to price. Usage rights say where the brand can use your video (organic posts or paid ads) and for how long (for example 30 or 90 days). Guides report creators adding about 20% to 50% of the base fee per month of paid use (example: a $200 video plus 30 days of ads adds about $40 to $100). Perpetual or unlimited use should cost the most. Before you sign, check three lines: where it runs, how long, and organic or paid.
