Animator story-style review render (26 Oct 2026). Not scheduled; needs owner OK.

## Instagram
Beginners usually charge $50-$400 per UGC video (Insense, Sep 2026). Brands pay for the work, not your follower count. Your base price covers a short, organic post; paid ads, more platforms or longer use cost more. Raw footage costs less than a finished, edited video. Get price, usage and deadline in writing before you film. The catch: rates swing a lot by niche, so read a few briefs before you quote.
