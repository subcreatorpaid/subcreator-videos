## Instagram
Etsy fees on a $10 template (US): $0.20 listing, 6.5% transaction, ~3% + $0.25 payment processing = you keep about $8.60. Example: 50 sales/month ≈ $430. Templates that sell solve one job (budget sheet, content calendar, media kit). Use buyers' words in titles, show it in use, make 5 listings. The catch: crowded niches; go narrow.
