TikTok Shop Lets You In At 1K. The Trap Is 5K

In the US, TikTok Shop affiliate takes you at 1,000 followers if you are 18+ and pass identity checks. The catch: sellers set the commission, returns can cancel it, and the rules change fast, so check your creator center. Use the pilot well: 5 products you really use, show each in use, tag every one.
