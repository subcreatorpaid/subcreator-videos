## Instagram
Patreon for new creators (since 4 Aug 2025): 10% platform fee + payment processing (US card 2.9% + $0.30). On a $10 membership you keep about $8.41. Example: 20 members = about $168/month before tax. Fans paying in another currency can add a 2.5% conversion fee. Start with one perk fans can't get free and one simple tier. The catch: a link alone won't sell; the perk has to be worth it.
