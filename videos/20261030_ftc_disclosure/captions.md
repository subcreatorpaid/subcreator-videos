## Instagram
Per the FTC's Disclosures 101 guide: put the disclosure in the post itself, not only in your bio or behind "more". In videos, say it and show it. Use clear words like "ad" or "sponsored", not "sp" or "collab". The platform's tool alone isn't enough. General info, not legal advice. Save this. ## Facebook
If you share links you earn from, the FTC's guidance is simple: make it obvious, in the post itself, in plain words. In video, say it out loud and put it on screen. General info, not legal advice.

## LinkedIn
The FTC's guidance for influencers applies to anyone who earns from a recommendation. Key points: disclose within the content, use plain language such as "ad", say and show it in video, and don't rely on platform tools alone. US rules can apply to posts made abroad that reach US consumers.
