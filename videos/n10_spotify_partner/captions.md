## Instagram
Spotify Gives Podcasters Half The Ad Money

The Spotify Partner Program pays podcasters 50% of the ad revenue from Spotify ads in their episodes. To qualify: host on Spotify for Creators, 3+ published episodes, plus 2,000 consumption hours and 1,000 audience in the last 30 days (with 30-minute episodes that is about 4,000 full listens a month). You only earn on episodes with at least one ad break. The catch: to stay eligible you must publish and earn at least $10 in any 6-month period. Open in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and dozens more markets (check Spotify's list).
