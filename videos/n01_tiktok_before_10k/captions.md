TikTok Pays At 10K. Here's What Pays Before

TikTok Creator Rewards needs 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in the last 30 days and age 18+, and reports put the pay at about $0.40 to $1 per 1,000 qualifying views (third-party figure). Three streams that pay before that: UGC (beginners usually charge $50 to $400 a video, brands judge samples, not followers), Amazon Associates (0 followers to join, but you need 3 qualifying sales in 180 days) and Etsy templates (you keep about $8.60 of a $10 sale in the US after the main fees). The catch: none of them is passive.
