Podcasts Pay Clippers. Here's The Real Rate

Clipping: podcast shows and networks pay clippers to post short moments from their episodes. Reported rates are about $0.80 to $3 per 1,000 verified views (observed ranges that can change), so a 50,000-view clip earns about $40 to $150. The catch: no views, no pay, and campaigns can change rates any time. After a few steady weeks some shows move clippers to a flat fee per clip.
