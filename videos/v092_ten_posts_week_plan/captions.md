## Instagram
A 10-posts-a-week plan: 6 teach posts (answer one viewer question), 3 tool posts (something you use, result on screen), 1 proof post (official numbers or honest results, never fake screenshots). Film in 2 batches a week and reuse one long video as 3 shorts. The catch: 10 weak posts lose to 5 good ones.
