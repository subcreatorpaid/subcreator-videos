## Instagram
Substack takes 10% of paid subscriptions + Stripe (~2.9% + $0.30). On a $5 subscription you keep about $4.05. Patreon also takes 10% for new creators; Ko-fi takes 0% on tips and 5% on memberships on its free plan. Example: 100 paid readers ≈ $405/month before tax. Start free, send one useful issue a week, switch on paid when readers ask. The catch: most readers stay free.
