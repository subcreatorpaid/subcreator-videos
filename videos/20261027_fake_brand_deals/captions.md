## Instagram
Before you reply to that "brand ambassador" DM, check these 5 signs the FTC flags: an unsolicited message, heavy flattery, name-dropping real staff, lookalike links, and a request for bank or personal details. Verify by contacting the brand through a number or address you find yourself. Save this. ## Facebook
Small creators get these messages a lot. The FTC's red flags: unsolicited "manager" DMs, flattery, name-dropping real staff, lookalike websites, and requests for bank or personal details. When unsure, contact the company directly using details you found yourself.

## LinkedIn
The FTC has warned about impersonators posing as brand ambassador managers to target aspiring influencers. The giveaway is a request for personal or bank details before any work begins. Verification is simple: reach the company through contact details you sourced independently.
