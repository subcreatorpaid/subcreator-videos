## Instagram
Brands pay for videos, not followers. It's called UGC: short videos you film that brands run as their own ads.

Where to find work: Billo (no follower minimum; US, UK, Canada), JoinBrands (free to join), or sell UGC as a Fiverr gig.
Pay: beginners usually charge $50-$400 a video (Insense, Sep 2026). Rates vary by niche and usage rights.
To get hired you need 3 samples filmed on your phone. Label them "not sponsored" and never imply a brand hired you.
The catch: no brand says yes until it sees your samples.
