# 5 terms to check before a brand deal

## Instagram
Before filming a brand deal, get 5 terms in writing: 1) price + what's included, 2) usage rights (where, how long), 3) deadline, 4) number of revisions, 5) payment date and method. Plus how you'll disclose it (FTC: clearly labelled, in the video). If it isn't written down, assume it isn't agreed.
