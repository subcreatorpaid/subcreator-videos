## Instagram
Price your first guide by the value to the buyer, not the hours you spent. Example: a checklist that saves an hour might be $5-10; a full system that saves a week can be more. Launch at one price: if almost everyone buys, raise it; if nobody does, explain it better or test lower. Remember fees (Gumroad: 10% + $0.50 direct, before card fees). Examples only.
